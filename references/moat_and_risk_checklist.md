# Economic Moat & Downside Risk Checklist

This document provides a reference checklist for evaluating competitive moats, management quality, and risk factors.

---

## 1. Economic Moat Evaluation (5 Pillar Moat Framework)
1. **Network Effects**: Does the value of the product increase exponentially as more users join? (e.g., platforms, payment networks).
2. **Switching Costs**: Is it expensive, painful, or risky for customers to switch to a competitor? (e.g., enterprise software, core banking).
3. **Cost Advantage**: Can the company produce goods/services at a lower cost than competitors due to scale, location, or unique assets?
4. **Intangible Assets**: Does the company possess strong pricing power driven by brand loyalty, patents, or regulatory licenses?
5. **Efficient Scale**: Is the market size limited such that only 1 or 2 dominant players can operate profitably?

---

## 2. Cyclicality & Value Trap Verification
Before finalizing a stable value thesis, verify it is not a **Value Trap**:
- **Peak Earnings Warning**: Is current low P/E driven by temporary peak commodity/industry cycle prices?
- **Technological Disruption Risk**: Is the business model facing structural decline (e.g., legacy print media, obsolete technology)?
- **Debt Refinancing Risk**: Does the company have heavy debt maturing within 12-24 months in a high-interest environment?

---

## 3. Management & Capital Allocation Check
- **Capital Allocation Track Record**:
  - Does management buy back shares when stock is undervalued, or at market peaks?
  - Does management engage in value-destroying M&A (empire building)?
- **Insider Ownership & Governance**:
  - Do executives hold significant skin-in-the-game (substantial share ownership)?
  - Are executive compensation incentives aligned with long-term Free Cash Flow per share growth?
