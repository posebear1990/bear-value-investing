# Stable Company Valuation & Shareholder Return Framework

This document outlines the analysis method for mature, cash-generative, and stable growth companies.

---

## 1. Key Valuation Multiples & Historical Context
- **P/E (Price-to-Earnings Ratio)**: Compare current P/E against the 5-year and 10-year historical percentile.
- **P/B (Price-to-Book Ratio)**: Useful for capital-heavy, financial, or asset-rich businesses. Combine with ROE (Return on Equity).
- **EV/EBITDA**: Useful for evaluating cash generation while neutralizing differences in capital structure and debt levels.

---

## 2. Balance Sheet Cash & Debt Breakdown
- **Net Cash Calculation**:
  $$\text{Net Cash} = (\text{Cash \& Cash Equivalents} + \text{Short-Term Investments}) - \text{Total Interest-Bearing Debt}$$
- **Net Cash to Market Cap Ratio**:
  $$\text{Net Cash Ratio} = \frac{\text{Net Cash}}{\text{Total Market Capitalization}}$$
  *If Net Cash Ratio > 20%-30%, the business provides a significant valuation cushion.*

---

## 3. Total Shareholder Return (TSR) Analysis
For stable companies, valuation relies on total capital returned to shareholders:

$$\text{Expected TSR} = \text{Dividend Yield} + \text{Share Buyback Yield} + \text{Organic Revenue/Profit Growth}$$

- **Dividend Yield**: $\frac{\text{Annual Dividend Per Share}}{\text{Current Stock Price}}$
- **Share Buyback Yield**: Annual reduction rate in total outstanding share count.
- **Organic Growth**: Long-term baseline volume or pricing growth (typically 2%-5%).

---

## 4. Dividend Safety & Payout Sustainability
Check whether dividend payments are safe and sustainable:
- **FCF Dividend Payout Ratio**:
  $$\text{FCF Payout Ratio} = \frac{\text{Total Dividends Paid}}{\text{Free Cash Flow}}$$
  *Safe threshold: Ideally < 70%. If > 90%, dividend cut risk increases if business faces headwinds.*
- **Debt Coverage**: Check if operating cash flow adequately covers annual debt maturities + interest expenses.
